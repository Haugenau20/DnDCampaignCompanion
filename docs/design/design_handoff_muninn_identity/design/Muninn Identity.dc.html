muninn.quest · identity touchpoints · turn 1

Where the name can live, without redoing the site

Muninn is one of Odin's two ravens. His name means memory. Every day he flies out over the world and comes back to report what he saw. For a shared record that the party reopens between sessions, the name already says what the product does. The rest of this page is about saying it in a few places, quietly.

Touchpoints, ordered by how often a player sees them

Your design language already sets the rule: ornament recedes as frequency rises. So the name gets the most room on the surfaces people see least. Rows, lists, filters and buttons stay exactly as they are.

Four angles

Each angle is shown on three existing surfaces (header, hero band, empty state), using your light-theme tokens. You can combine them. My suggestion is at the bottom.

1a

The raven

The literal reading: a raven becomes the mark. People recognise it instantly, and it makes the strongest app icon.

Works because
You can recognise it at 16px. Your design language says to use shipped public-domain plates first, and old manuscript drawings of Odin's ravens are exactly that.
Watch out for
You need real artwork; a clip-art raven turns it into a costume quickly. Ravens are also common in fantasy branding.

The return

A structural angle. Muninn flies out and comes back with a report. The home hero becomes that report: what changed since you last played.

My suggestion

1c for the mark and 1b for the voice, used on the favicon, header, hero eyebrow, empty states, sign-in and 404. That's about six edits, no new colours, and no artwork to commission. Keep 1d as a later feature, and keep 1a for the sign-in plate if you find a good public-domain raven.

Small extras

Huginn for the AI. The other raven is Thought. The note parser that “pulls out NPCs, locations and quests” could be called Huginn. Thought finds things, Memory keeps them.
.quest as a verb. Show “muninn.quest” in full only in the footer, link previews and invite emails. The header stays “Muninn”.
Pronunciation. Players will say it aloud at the table. Put “MOO-nin” once, on the signed-out home or the about page.
Loading. The mark at rest, with no flapping or looping. That follows your motion rule.

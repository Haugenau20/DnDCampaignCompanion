Contact form: let people attach a screenshot to a bug report.
    - Deliberately left out of the contact page redesign (PR 2, 2026-08-30) rather than shipped
      as a disabled control that does nothing. The design mock (screenshot 5a) has the drop zone.
    - Why it is worth doing: for a bug report a screenshot is the single biggest quality win.
      "Deleting a note takes me back to the list" is three exchanges of questions without one.
    - Why it was deferred: it is not a UI change. It needs Firebase Storage wiring, security
      rules scoped so one user cannot read another's uploads, size and MIME-type limits, cleanup
      of orphaned uploads when a submission is abandoned, and a new failure mode on submit
      (upload succeeded, email failed, or the reverse). None of that is otherwise touched by the
      redesign.
    - Where it would go: src/shared/components/contact/ - a new field component alongside
      CategoryChips/SenderIdentity, plus a download URL in the callable payload that
      firebase/functions/src/contact.ts links from the email body.
